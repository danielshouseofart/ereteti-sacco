Ordering

How do I start?

Book an intake call on the contact page. After the call we send your menu and price, and you confirm it before anything is cooked.

Can I change my plan mid-week?

You can swap single meals before Thursday's cut-off. A full plan change takes effect the following week.

Can I pause for a week?

Yes. Message us before Thursday at 6pm and you won't be charged for the following week.

Delivery

Where do you deliver?

Westlands, Lavington, Kilimani and South B for now. More routes are planned for 2027.

What if I'm not home?

Our riders can leave your order with a caretaker or at a reception desk. Just tell us where when you sign up.

Food and health

How long do the meals keep?

Five days in the fridge. Each container has its date on the label, so you know what to eat first.

Do you cater for allergies?

Yes. Every plan is checked against the allergies you give us before it's cooked, not after.

Is the managed sugar plan medical advice?

No. It's a meal plan written with a nutritionist. If you have diabetes, keep following your doctor's advice and let us know about any dietary instructions they've given you.

Payment

How do I pay?

By M-Pesa, weekly and in advance. We text you the paybill number and your account reference when you confirm your plan.

Do you give refunds?

If a delivery is missed or a meal isn't what you ordered, tell us within 24 hours and we'll credit it to next week.

Still have a question?

Reach us on the contact page and we'll get back to you the same day.
