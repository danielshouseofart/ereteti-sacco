From sign-up to your first delivery

  1. Book a call A nutritionist phones you for about fifteen minutes to ask about your goals, allergies and what you already like to eat.
  2. We build your week Our head chef turns that call into a seven-day plan and sends you the full menu and price before anything is cooked.
  3. You approve it Swap out any meal you don't fancy. Nothing goes on the stove until you confirm the week.
  4. We deliver Your meals arrive chilled and labelled by day, in the window for your area.
  5. Reheat and eat Every lid has simple reheating steps. Most meals take under five minutes on the stove or in a microwave.

Storing and reheating

In the fridge

Put the whole week in the fridge as soon as it arrives. Each meal keeps for five days, and the date is on the label.

Reheating

Peel back the lid corner and microwave for two to three minutes, or warm it in a pan with a splash of water.

Freezing

Stews and soups freeze well for up to a month. Leave rice and fish dishes in the fridge.

Delivery days

All meals for the week arrive in a single Monday delivery, chilled and ready for the days ahead.
