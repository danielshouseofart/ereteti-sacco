Weekly plans

Prices cover seven days of meals, delivered once a week. You can pause or cancel any plan before the Thursday cut-off.

Plans and weekly prices
Plan Meals per day Best for Price per week
Balance 3 Everyday healthy eating KES 4,500
Lean & active 3 Training and weight management KES 5,200
Managed sugar 3 Diabetes and pre-diabetes KES 5,500
Family (4 people) 3 Households of four KES 14,000

What every plan includes

  • A fifteen-minute intake call with a nutritionist
  • A full menu you can edit before we cook
  • Chilled delivery in Westlands, Lavington, Kilimani and South B
  • Labelled containers with reheating steps on the lid
  • Free pausing before the Thursday cut-off

Paying with M-Pesa

  1. Confirm your plan Approve the weekly menu we send you after your intake call.
  2. Get the payment details We text you our paybill number and your account reference.
  3. Pay before Thursday 6pm Send the weekly amount by M-Pesa. Payment is in advance, every week.
  4. Get your confirmation You'll get an SMS once we receive it, and your meals go into the kitchen schedule.

Pausing a week

Pause any plan up to the Thursday cut-off with no charge for the following week.
