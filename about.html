Why we started FreshBytes

It began with a frustration most of us in Nairobi know. Eating well either means cooking everything yourself after a long day, or ordering food that has no plan behind it. We wanted a third option: meals built around a real nutrition plan, cooked by people who know food, arriving on a schedule you can keep.

We're a small team with a head chef, a nutrition lead and a delivery lead, plus the riders who cover our routes. Every plan is checked by someone on the team before it reaches your door.

What we stand for

Fresh

Ingredients come from local markets and go straight into the pot. If it isn't in season, it isn't on the menu.

Personal

Your allergies, your goals and your taste shape the plan. We don't hand everyone the same box.

Reliable

Same day, same window, every week. Skipping a week is one message before the Thursday cut-off.
